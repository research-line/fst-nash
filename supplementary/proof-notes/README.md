# Supplementary Proof Notes

This folder holds a **curated selection of individual, completed proof notes**
supporting the published paper. It is not the project's full internal proof
record: notes are released one file at a time, and only when all of the
following hold.

1. **Terminal step** — proven, refuted or otherwise closed; no open construction site.
2. **Covered by the published paper** — the claim's priority rests with the paper (DOI below).
3. **No forward references** to unpublished, ongoing research directions.
4. **Privacy-clean** — author initials only, no internal paths or infrastructure references.

Notes are published with their original wording and date; they are historical
documents, not living text. Where a note's conclusion was later qualified, the
published paper is authoritative.
